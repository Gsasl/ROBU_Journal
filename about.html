01 Path to Automation (LFR Workshop)
Organized by the Arts & Design and Technical teams, this hands-on workshop equips students with fundamental robotics concepts, sensor calibration (IR arrays), motor drivers (L298N/TB6612FNG), PID control loops, and microcontroller programming for smart Line-Following Robots.
02 Soccer Bot Workshop
Hosted by the Event Management Department with expert guest instructors, focusing on mechanical chassis design, wireless RF/Bluetooth communication, differential drive mechanics, and tactical strategy for competitive 1v1 robotic soccer tournaments.
03 LaTeX for Research & Academic Resumes
Conducted by senior club researchers and alumni, utilizing Overleaf to train members in typesetting IEEE paper templates, automated citation management (BibTeX), mathematical formulation, and high-impact CV formatting.
04 GitHub & Career Optimization Bootcamp
Foundational bootcamps on Git version control, branch management, open-source pull requests, alongside HR-led professional development seminars covering LinkedIn networking, portfolio design, and technical interview preparation.
05 Embedded Hardware & Firmware Engineering
An intensive industrial-grade program delving into STM32 32-bit ARM Cortex microcontrollers, bare-metal C programming, RTOS task scheduling, UART/SPI/I2C communication protocols, and PCB schematic design in EasyEDA/KiCAD.

Corporate-Style Progression Track

At ROBU, every new recruit embarks on a structured leadership roadmap. You begin by learning technical & organizational fundamentals and systematically climb to executive panel management.

1. Apprentice (AP)

Entry-level recruit stage. Hands-on learning through departmental bootcamps, shadow assignments, and workshop participation.

2. Executive Body

Progression through Junior Executive → Executive → Senior Executive. Operational ownership over event execution, software tools, design, and budget management.

3. Sub-Panel Leadership

Roles: Secretary → Assistant Director (AD) → Director. Strategic leadership over functional departments (SP, F&M, HR, EM, A&D, IT, E&P, RPM).

4. Core Panel & Advisory Board

Highest executive leadership: President, Vice President, General Secretary, guided by the Faculty Advisor. Directing club strategy, global sponsorships, and university relations.
